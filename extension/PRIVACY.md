# Sink for Chrome: privacy

The extension talks to one place: the Sink server you sign in to, which you run yourself.

- **What it sends there:** the notes and todos you write in it, and, only when you clip, the page you clipped (its
  address and content) or the text you selected.
- **What it keeps in the browser:** your server's address, its sign-in token, the folder new notes go in, and which
  note you had open.
- **What it reads:** a page's content only when you clip it, add a selection or add a link, and only that tab.
- Nothing goes to the extension's authors or anyone else. No analytics, no tracking, no ads, nothing sold.

Signing out revokes the token on your server and forgets it here.
